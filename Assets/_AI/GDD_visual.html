01 — EXPERIENCE VISION
What is Krazy Kart?

"Imagine a mall designed by a stunt choreographer and approved by a cartoon physicist."

Core tone mandate

Krazy Kart is an arcade runner where destruction is currency, style is strategy, and every run teaches you something new. Players ascend a procedurally generated mall from basement parking to the manager's office — leaving chaos in their wake and banking time for the final rooftop escape.

💥
Playful Anarchy
Nothing is off-limits. Everything can be destroyed. Everyone overreacts.
🎭
Physical Comedy
Ragdolls, flying trays, and tumbling appliances. The physics engine IS the joke.
🎮
Controlled Absurdity
Chaotic visually, readable mechanically. Every element serves gameplay.
🏆
Skill Expression
Casual players have fun. Experts get significantly more time and higher scores.
02 — CORE LOOP
Every Run, Every Time

A single run lasts ~15 minutes. Every floor is immediately accessible — but performance in each floor directly determines how much escape time you bank for the final sequence. The loop rewards replayability: the same floors feel different based on your chosen playstyle.

01
Basement Spawn
Kart launches into B1 parking. Physics established. First boost.
02
Floor Ascent
6–8 floors. Choose your style per floor: rush, destroy, or showboat.
03
Earn Mall Credit
Destruction, style combos, NPC hits and cop evasions convert to MC.
04
MC → Time Buffer
Total MC converts to escape time. More MC = more room to breathe.
05
Boss Floor
Manager's office. Environment becomes hostile. Shutters drop. Clock ticks.
06
Glass Smash Exit
Boost runway. Full speed. Glass shatters. Cinematic slow-mo. Ragdoll glory.
Key Design Insight: All floors are accessible immediately — no unlock gates. This means the player always feels in control. The consequence of "easy" floors is less escape time in the boss sequence. Difficulty emerges from the player's own choices, not gating.
03 — DIFFICULTY PHILOSOPHY
You Choose How Hard

Krazy Kart has no explicit difficulty slider. Instead, the player's playstyle determines how challenging the boss escape will be. Interaction-heavy runs bank more MC and more escape time. Speed runs are short but brutal. Stylish runs find the optimal balance.

04 — PRIMARY CURRENCY
The Mall Credit System

Mall Credit (MC) is the game's single performance resource. It is simultaneously your score, your currency, and your survival mechanism. Every destructive, stylish, or evasive action earns MC. At the end of all floors, your total MC converts directly into seconds of escape time.

Diegetically: MC is your mall reputation score, security bribe fund, black-market shopping credit, and insurance liability rating — all in one.

Mall Credit Earned Escape Time Buffer Difficulty Feel Time Bar
0 MC 12 sec Brutal — nearly impossible
200 MC 20 sec Very hard — no margin for error
500 MC 35 sec Hard — one or two mistakes allowed
900 MC 55 sec Manageable — casual-friendly
1400 MC 85 sec Comfortable — stylish exit
Design Intent: The time buffer represents security lockdown delay, shutter closing speed, and trap activation timing. The more MC you've banked, the slower the mall "reacts" to your escape — giving you room to maneuver, attempt stunts, and style your way out. Low MC = maximum hostility from frame one.

FLOOR LAYOUT PRINCIPLES
GUARANTEED IN EVERY FLOOR
  • At least 2 viable navigation paths to the exit
  • One open flow zone (wide, boost-friendly)
  • One destruction cluster (dense props, high MC)
  • One optional vertical route (stunt/shortcut)
  • Procedural guarantee: exit always reachable
VERTICALITY EXAMPLES
  • Ramps from fallen shelves or toppled fridges
  • Mezzanine balconies via escalator drift
  • Half-pipe toy displays and skateboard ramps
  • Stacked crate pyramids and showroom platforms
  • Secret ceiling shortcuts for expert players
06 — TRANSITION CORRIDORS
The Spiral System

Inspired by Tokyo Drift parking spirals — C-shaped transition corridors connect every floor. They serve three critical purposes: breathing space between chaos arenas, streaming buffer for the next level, and a showcase moment for drift skill. Clean lines through the spiral reward boost chaining.

DESIGN GOALS
Between Every Floor
Encourage drifting. Allow boost chaining. Reward clean lines. No obstacles — just momentum and style. The kart should feel amazing to drive through these sections.
VISUAL THEMES
Parking spiral (B1 → F1)
Service corridor (F1 → F2)
Delivery ramp (F2 → F3)
Glass elevator shaft (F3 → F4)
Maintenance tunnel (F5 → F6)
Emergency stairwell (F7 → Boss)

08 — COP KART SYSTEM
Security Always Escalates

Cops are not random spawn events — they are a direct response to the player's chaos output. Higher destruction = more cop karts = more pressure. The system prevents passive play and keeps all playstyles engaged. Importantly, evading or eliminating cops is one of the highest MC yields in the game.

THREAT BEHAVIOURS
  • Box-in attempts — two karts flank the player and converge
  • Ram pressure — direct high-speed collision attempts
  • Lane blocking — cops position ahead to redirect into obstacles
  • Herding — cops work in formation to funnel toward walls
  • Ambush spawns — appearing from side doors after high destruction
  • Ramp denial — cops position at stunt route entrances
PLAYER COUNTER OPTIONS
  • Outmaneuver — use superior boost and drift to lose them
  • Environmental knock — drift into a cop and send it into a shelf
  • Verticality escape — ramps cops cannot navigate
  • Physics toy traps — ball pits, swinging signs, rolling carts
  • Cop-on-cop redirect — angle so two cops collide with each other
  • NPC crowd scatter — using a crowd to break cop formation
Spawn Thresholds (Proposed): First cop at 3+ destroyed props per floor. Second cop at 6+ destructions OR one NPC ragdoll. Third cop in formation at 10+ destructions. Maximum active karts: 4. Cops despawn at floor transition — clean slate each level.
09 — BOSS FLOOR
The Manager's Office
FINAL ESCAPE SEQUENCE
Tone: Sterile. Premium. Minimal clutter. Maximum danger.

The boss floor is a dramatic tonal contrast to the chaos below. Where floors are loud and colourful, the office is clinical and white — until it isn't. The player's banked MC is now their survival runway. The environment becomes progressively hostile over time.

PHASE 01
Clean Entry
First 8 seconds. Clear floor. Only obstacle is the sterile layout. Shutters visible but open. Clock ticking.
PHASE 02
Lockdown Begins
Shutters start closing. Glass partitions slide into position. Security bollards deploy from the floor.
PHASE 03
Active Hostility
Floor-cleaning robots create slippery paths. Rotating doors form timing puzzles. Escalating hazard density.
PHASE 04
Glass Runway
Long corridor opens. Player builds full boost. Glass wall at the end. Cinematic slow-motion ragdoll exit. Victory laundry.
Cinematic Exit: At full speed, the kart hits the glass wall at the end of the corridor. Time slows to 20%. The kart ragdolls through in a shower of glass. The ragdoll "flaps dramatically like victory laundry" as the building falls away behind it. Run complete.
10 — LEADERBOARD STRUCTURE
Six Ways to Be the Best

The leaderboard celebrates all playstyles. Speedrunners, destroyers, stylish drivers, and minimalist runners all have a category to chase. No single approach dominates all boards — community diversity is a designed outcome.

⚡
Fastest Escape Time
Raw speedrun category. Minimal MC, maximum precision. Clock ends on ragdoll exit.
💰
Highest Mall Credit
Total MC accumulated across all floors. Rewards destruction and style equally.
🔗
Longest Combo Chain
Maximum number of consecutive actions without breaking the streak. Technique-focused.
🧊
Least Destruction Run
Successful escape with the minimum props destroyed. Ghost-mode challenge.
💥
Max Destruction Run
Most props destroyed in a successful escape. Chaos maximalist category.
🎨
Style Score Run
Weighted score: MC × combo multiplier average × stunt count. Showboater's crown.
11 — ART DIRECTION & TECHNICAL NOTES
Look, Feel & Build
ART DIRECTION
  • Hyper-stylized casual look — not realistic, not cartoon. In between.
  • Chunky props, slightly exaggerated scale, soft rounded edges everywhere.
  • Bright plastics, saturated packaging, maximum silhouette readability.
  • Destruction readability over realism — props break clearly, not subtly.
  • Ragdoll exaggeration encouraged: comedy over physics accuracy.
  • Physical comedy is the primary visual language.
UI PHILOSOPHY
  • Minimal clutter. The floor is the experience.
  • Bottom left: mini map showing current floor layout.
  • Top right: Mall Credit counter (animated on earn).
  • Centre: lightweight objective hints only.
  • Boss floor: countdown timer becomes dominant, full-screen tension.
  • Combo streak shown as a satisfying escalating counter.
PROCEDURAL TECH REQUIREMENTS
  • Layout graph approach: nodes are module types, edges are connections.
  • Guaranteed navigable path from entry to exit on every generation.
  • Vertical routes optional — never block the main path.
  • Object density scales with floor number (B1 sparse → F8 dense).
  • Cop spawn zones pre-baked into layout, not random placement.
  • Streaming buffer: next floor generates during transition corridor.
VERTICAL SLICE MUST PROVE
  • Kart controls feel great from first second — responsive, weighty, fun.
  • Destruction is visually rewarding and mechanically satisfying.
  • Procedural layout is replayable — same floor type, different feel.
  • Boss escape sequence generates genuine tension and payoff.
  • MC system communicates clearly — player always knows why they earned.
  • 15-minute session length feels complete and not padded.
